These terms explain use of the Hestia installation at hestia.klatt.ie, operated by Mateusz Klatt. Contact: mateusz@klatt.ie. It is a private, self-hosted household application for the operator and invited users, not an offer of a public SaaS, banking or payment service.

Use this installation only if the operator has authorised your access, and follow these terms and the permissions assigned to your account. If you operate your own copy of the open-source software, you are responsible for that separate installation and its users.

Read-only banking access is optional and off by default. Hestia contains an Enable Banking budget module, which runs only after the operator explicitly enables it and only for accounts their holder has authorised at the bank and that are explicitly chosen for import or synchronisation. These terms do not themselves connect a bank account or authorise access.

1. Current service and optional integrations

Hestia provides local smart-home device monitoring and control, household automations, user accounts and preferences, activity logs and optional synchronisation of selected Google and Apple/iCloud calendars with a local SQLite calendar. Optional features can include outdoor weather data and additional local device integrations.

Available functions depend on the installed version, hardware, configuration and your role. Administrators manage users and settings, operators can perform permitted control and editing actions, and viewers have read access to the information exposed to their role. A role does not imply access to an external account that its owner has not authorised.

Standalone mode replaces the upstream vendor device service. Proxy mode relays device traffic to that upstream service. Separately enabled integrations may contact their providers in either mode.

2. Responsible use and account security

  • Connect and control only devices, calendars and accounts that you own or are authorised to use. Respect other household members and people mentioned in shared data.
  • Keep application credentials, session tokens and integration secrets private. Do not bypass permissions, probe other users' accounts or use this installation to send malicious traffic. Report suspected misuse or an exposed credential to the operator.
  • Check device actions and automation rules before relying on them. Hestia is not designed as an emergency or safety-critical service. Automated commands can affect real equipment and should be tested and supervised where appropriate.
  • Do not place unnecessary sensitive information in event notes, device names, diagnostic reports or support messages.

3. Calendar synchronisation

Synchronisation is opt-in and limited to the calendars selected in a configured group. Adding, editing or deleting an event in a connected calendar or Hestia can change the other copies. Only link a shared calendar if the necessary sharing permissions are in place.

Not every provider feature or event format is portable. Provider restrictions, concurrent edits, unavailable networks or expired authorisation can delay or prevent delivery. Check synchronisation status and important events, and keep suitable backups before substantial changes. Hestia's record is not a guarantee that a provider has completed a write.

Deleting a calendar event marks the local record inactive and queues deletion of connected copies. Event payloads, mappings and permanent deletion records can remain in SQLite to prevent older copies from restoring the event. Disconnecting a calendar is not the same as deleting its local history or provider-side data. The Privacy Policy explains retention and removal requests.

4. Optional read-only banking access

The optional budget module lets an individual authorise access to their own linked accounts through Enable Banking for private, non-commercial household budgeting. It reads permitted balances and transactions for the chosen accounts, or imports history from an earlier authorised download, and stores them on this self-hosted server, as described in the Privacy Policy. Only the budget's owner can view it in Hestia. Bank coverage, transaction history, card support and data freshness depend on the bank, the provider and their access limits.

Local budget reports use the owner's currently selected accounts and keep currencies separate. Categories, literal classification rules and reviewed own-account transfer relationships can change how those records are grouped in reports; they do not edit the bank's records. Transfer suggestions require the owner's acceptance. Missing, stale or uncertain records and unresolved transfer choices can affect the usefulness of totals.

Hestia does not initiate transfers or payments, hold money or ask you to give it bank login passwords, PINs or one-time banking codes. Complete bank authentication only through the bank and Enable Banking's authorisation flow. Do not paste banking credentials into Hestia, a support request or a source-code repository.

The regulated account information service is provided by Enable Banking under its separate terms, authorisation and privacy arrangements. Its free restricted-production arrangement is subject to its current rules for the Control Panel user's own linked accounts. It does not grant permission for access to somebody else's accounts, professional use or a public banking service. See Enable Banking's application terms and restricted account linking.

You can revoke the bank/provider consent, pause a connection or ask the operator to disconnect access. Doing so stops future collection but does not automatically erase previously stored local data. Local deletion is handled separately as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Balances, transaction lists and any spending summaries are organisational aids, not financial advice or a promise that funds will be available. They can be delayed or incomplete, and pending transactions may not appear. Check actual bank balances and payment deadlines before making a financial decision.

5. Data handling and external services

The Privacy Policy describes server-side storage, cookies, activity logs, optional integrations and your choices. Self-hosting does not mean browser-only processing. This deployment uses Cloudflare and an Apache reverse proxy for delivery and security, and enabled providers process information needed for their services.

Google, Apple, Enable Banking and other external services have their own terms. You must have the right permissions for your use of them. Their availability, eligibility rules, charges and data-access conditions are separate from this household installation.
