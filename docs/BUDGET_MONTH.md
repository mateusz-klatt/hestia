## Computation

### Actual income, spending and limits

| Case | Dashboard treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Start budget | Sum actual credits of effective kind `income` whose stable category IDs are configured. Initialize with both salaries in `Wynagrodzenie` and the child benefit in `Świadczenia ZUS`. Credits on the 28th enter the actual budget when booked and visible, and they replace their corresponding estimate. Other income, unclassified credits and refunds stay outside start budget. `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:157–172,510–525`, `hestia/budget/analytics_core.py:171–173` |
| Spending and refund | Sum effective `expense` debits minus effective `refund` credits, in the refund's credited category on its own booking/value date. An uncategorised refund reduces the uncategorised bucket. A refund of a prior-month purchase reduces this month's spend and can make a category or day negative. Preserve that sign. Use the special treatment below for the designated mortgage category. `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:510–542` |
| Own transfer and card repayment | Exclude both legs of a currently accepted projected transfer, even across month boundaries. A card purchase is a separate expense on its purchase date. Suggested, stale, ambiguous and selection-boundary pairs retain their existing effective classification and may affect totals until reviewed. Surface a review hint when the month has unresolved candidates or a limited transfer scan. `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:269–297,458–497,504–507,670–679` |
| Krypto | The owner classifies these rows with effective kind `excluded` using rules or annotations. Exclude that kind on both debit and credit from all dashboard income and spend series. Category labels alone carry no kind. `hestia/db.py:479–489`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:157–172,510–525` |
| Category change mid-month | Recompute the whole month and displayed history against current selection, rules, annotations and income settings. Group by stable ID and use the current name, including archived categories already in use. A rename keeps identity. Reclassification can change totals. Treat an uncategorised bucket as its own group. `hestia/budget/analytics_store.py:136–177`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:148–172,228–250,530–542` |

### Rent and one mortgage

Configure an optional pair of distinct, dedicated categories. For this household use `Najem` for effective `income` credits and a dedicated expense category for the specific mortgage debit. The owner creates the precise rules or annotations in the existing UI. Mortgage schedules and paid flags are reminders, with no ledger-to-schedule link. Amount or label similarity does not select a mortgage. Reserve the rent category outside budget income by design and disallow it in the income list. `hestia/db.py:479–489,517–585`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:157–172,817–839`, `hestia/budget/analytics_store.py:368–403`

For each month/currency, let `R` be reserved rent credited, `M` the designated mortgage debits and `F` refunds categorised to that mortgage. Use `mortgage_net = max(M−F, 0)` and `matched = min(R, mortgage_net)`. Show both legs, refunds, matched amount, `uncovered_mortgage = mortgage_net−matched` and `unapplied_rent = R−matched`. Fully funded rent and mortgage contribute zero to start budget and spending. Unapplied rent stays reserved. Recognize any uncovered amount as mortgage spending. Allocate `matched` to mortgage debits in purchase-date then local transaction-key order. Recognize up to `M−matched` of mortgage refunds on their credit dates, in credit-date then key order. Show any refund excess on the pair only. This keeps daily, category and monthly net spending reconciled. A later rent credit can reduce earlier spending on the same month's chart. If one leg is absent, display the observed leg and the gap. Keep each month and currency independent. `hestia/budget/analytics_core.py:63–86`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:499–542`

### Expected income and pace

Use last calendar month's eligible credits, grouped by income category, currency and receiving account. The current recurring detector accepts expense debits only. For a group with one prior credit per normalized debtor name and with every current credit matching a prior name, expect each missing stream at its prior exact amount. Normalize the structured debtor name with whitespace collapsing and case folding, and use it only inside the calculation. A qualifying credit consumes its own stream's forecast regardless of amount or day, so a second salary can stay expected after the first arrives. Use the prior day of month clamped to this month's length as the estimated day. If it passed, show that the credit is still expected. `hestia/budget/recurring.py:152–166,179–200`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:175–193,328–349,546–553`

If names are missing, a prior stream repeats, or a current payer is unmatched, use the prior group total with an unset due day while the group has zero current credits. Once any current credit in an ambiguous group posts, suppress that group's estimate. Mark evidence incomplete if a configured category in the selected currency has no prior qualifying credits or any of its groups used an ambiguous fallback. Show any available subtotal as partial in that case. Use `unavailable` when all applicable groups lack evidence and `none` when evidence shows no pending income or no categories are configured. Display only category, amount, optional expected date and source. Past months have booked income only. `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:107–117,175–193,499–501,546–553`, `hestia/budget/analytics_core.py:101–112`

For the current month let `S` be net spend to date, `d` the Warsaw day of month and `D` the number of days in the month. The projection budget `B` is actual start budget plus any available pending estimate. Label the basis as received or received plus expected, including any partial-evidence marker. If `S > 0`, return pace `S/d` and projected end spend `S×D/d`. The first estimated exhaustion day is the smallest `n` in `d…D` with `S×n >= B×d`. If `B <= 0`, report no funded budget. Otherwise report already reached when `S >= B`, no positive pace when `S <= 0`, exhaustion during the month when a day qualifies, or within budget at this pace. The actual headroom uses booked income. Past months show actual spend and no forward pace. Exact comparisons precede display rounding. `hestia/budget/analytics_core.py:38–112`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:510–525`

### Credit-card exposure

Report an **estimated unpaid share of this month's card spending**, current month only. Require a selected, explicitly confirmed `credit_card`, an unambiguous live same-currency closing booked balance (`CLBD` with a reference date in the current month through today), and a successful transaction acquisition on or after that Warsaw date. Use the existing component stale thresholds. Enable the estimate for a provider only once its booked balance sign convention is confirmed. Under the debit-negative convention use `max(−booked_balance, 0)`. Ignore available balance, credit limit, archived snapshots and product-name heuristics. Missing or stale inputs, an unsupported convention, or a known uncertain or unresolved booked entry in the allocation window make the card's estimate unavailable. Probe such entries across the full card scope through the reference date, including a potentially relevant undated booked observation seen in the current month. Use an existence check, independent of Quarantine's display cap. A fresh acquisition time establishes observation recency. The allocation remains an estimate because the stored window and card disclosures do not establish statement coverage. `hestia/db.py:278–297,314–325`, `hestia/budget/normalize.py:136–149`, `hestia/budget/store.py:27,450–472,520–547,683–707`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:1237–1257`, `hestia/budget/views.py:207–230`, `docs/BUDGET.md:35–40`, `ui/src/budget/budget.tsx:69–70`

Allocate the observed debt newest booked debit first, equivalent to assuming repayments clear oldest debt first. Walk eligible same-card/currency debits booked from month start through the reference date, in reverse booking/value-date order with a stable reference tie-break. Allocate up to each debit until the debt is exhausted. Count a slice toward exposure only if the debit is a budget expense with a purchase date in the current month. Use its post-pass-through expense as the upper bound for that slice. Other debits can absorb debt without entering the exposure total. Earlier unpaid debt can absorb any unallocated remainder. Posted repayments and refunds already affect the booked balance. Display the oldest contributing balance date and partial coverage when some eligible cards lack inputs. With no usable card, return unavailable, or `no_cards` when there is no selected confirmed card for the currency. A known zero balance can produce a zero estimate. Prior months have unavailable exposure. `hestia/budget/analytics_core.py:63–98`, `hestia/budget/analytics_views.py:175–193,218–240,458–476`, `hestia/budget/views.py:207–230`
